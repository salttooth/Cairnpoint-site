Practical, not theoretical

Helping teams decide what to do next

Accessibility requirements are rarely the hard part. The harder questions are operational: what to prioritize, when media needs remediation, how audio description fits into real workflows, and how limited budgets can produce the most meaningful access.

I help institutional teams turn accessibility obligations into clear decisions, repeatable processes, and implementation plans people can actually use.
