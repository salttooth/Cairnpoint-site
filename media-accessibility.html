Media Accessibility

Media Accessibility Consulting

Media accessibility is rarely as simple as caption everything and move on. Teams are often managing large video libraries, evolving requirements, competing priorities, and limited resources. I help organizations determine what requires attention, how to prioritize efforts, and how to develop media accessibility practices that hold up over time.

Making media accessibility decisions that hold up

  • Captioning practices and workflows
  • Video library assessments
  • Media remediation prioritization
  • Archive considerations
  • Vendor and service evaluation
  • Workflow improvement opportunities
  • Guidance on balancing accessibility, risk, and available resources
