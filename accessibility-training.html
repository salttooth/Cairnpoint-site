Accessibility Training and Workshops

CairnPoint provides practical workshops for teams that need to understand accessibility requirements, make better decisions, and implement accessible media practices.

What this supports

Training built around real team decisions

Effective accessibility training should help people do their work differently. CairnPoint workshops focus on practical implementation, decision-making, and workflows rather than abstract compliance concepts alone.

  • Customized workshops for higher education, healthcare, research, and nonprofit teams
  • Accessibility awareness sessions grounded in real examples
  • Practical implementation guidance for teams responsible for digital media
  • Accessibility decision making for prioritization, risk, and workflow planning
  • Media accessibility training for captions, audio description, and video accessibility
