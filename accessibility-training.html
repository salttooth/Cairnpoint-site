Accessibility Training and Workshops

Many accessibility trainings explain requirements. Teams often leave still wondering how those requirements apply to their day-to-day work. These workshops focus on practical implementation — helping participants understand not only what accessibility expectations are, but how to make informed decisions within the realities of their roles.

What this supports

Training built around real team decisions

  • Media accessibility fundamentals
  • Audio description in practice
  • Prioritizing accessibility work
  • Decision-making frameworks
  • Accessibility considerations for content creators
  • Building sustainable processes
  • Discussion of real-world scenarios
