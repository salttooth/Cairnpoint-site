Accessibility requirements are rarely the hard part. The harder questions are operational: how higher education, healthcare, research, and nonprofit teams should prioritize work, plan accessible media, make audio description decisions, and build processes that can last.

CairnPoint helps institutional teams turn accessibility obligations into clear decisions, repeatable workflows, and implementation plans people can actually use.
